# The near-field ring fit: rendered views, then a 2 m hold in strong wind

Measured on 20 Nov 2026 (UTC) on a club laptop, on `platform/build-2027` at `75060e5` plus the CEN-13 change. Sim image `argus-sim:humble`.

**Result.** On rendered views of the bullseye from 1.5 to 4 m, tilted up to 10°, blurred and noisy, with the target partly out of the picture, the ring fit found the centre in 60 of 60, within 0.6 px at the median and 3 px at worst. In Gazebo, the 2 m hold in strong wind over the full 1.5 m bullseye, which lost its target 30 to 40 times without the ring fit, never lost it with it, and the vehicle's true error at the 95th percentile fell from 0.31 m to 0.20 m.

## Rendered views

`src/drone_target_cv/test/test_ring_fit.py` renders the sim's two bullseyes (5 m with eight rings, 1.5 m with four) as the sim's downward camera sees them (640 x 360, 389 px focal length) with `tools/synth_targets.py`, and fits each.

| Views | Found | Centre error |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8: level, 1.5, 2.4, 3.0 and 4.0 m, both targets | 8 of 8 | under 1.0 px each |
| 4: the 5 m target at 2.4 m, its centre up to 280 px from the picture's centre (the target is 810 px across in a 640 px picture) | 4 of 4 | under 1.5 px each |
| 12: tilted 8 to 14° in all, blur 1.2 px, noise 6 levels, at 1.5, 2.4 and 4.0 m, levelled with the camera's attitude | 12 of 12 | under 2.0 px each |
| 60: random height 1.5 to 4 m, tilt up to 10° about each axis, centre up to 200 px by 120 px off, blur 1.0 px, noise 5 levels, both targets | 60 of 60 | median under 0.6 px, 57th of 60 under 1.5 px, worst under 3.0 px |

Declined, as they should be: a centre outside the picture; plain ground; one red disc; red stripes; two discs side by side; a 1.5 m eight-ring target from 30 m (rings 1.2 px wide). The same 60 views fitted as ellipses with a free centre, the first design, were found in 39 of 60 at 77 ms a frame; levelled first and fitted as circles, 60 of 60 at 16 ms.

## In Gazebo: the hold the box detector could not keep

`drop/hold_2m_ring_fit`: the 2026 drop mission made to hold at 2 m for 20 s on its centring, wind 7 m/s from 225° with 2 m/s gusts and one of 3 m/s, the 1.5 m four-ring bullseye. Flags `use_frame_time_pose`, `use_target_estimator`, `centre_on_estimator`, `centring_retune`, `use_ring_fit`; node flags `target_cv.stamp_from_image`, `target_cv.detection_contract`, `ring_fit.ring_fit`. One flight, seed 1.

| | Without the ring fit (three flights while CEN-02 was tuned, same scenario otherwise) | With the ring fit (the code as committed) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Run | OK, OK, TIMEOUT | OK |
| Hold error, p95 | 0.310 m, 0.301 m, 0.281 m | 0.200 m |
| Hold error, median; max | 0.114 m; 0.43 m (first flight) | 0.069 m; 0.275 m |
| Time at the hold height before 20 s centred in a row | 151 s, 149 s, 160 s | 47 s (3 starts of the 20 s) |
| "track is LOST" log lines (one per 2 s) | 34, 41, 31 | 0 |
| "not descending: track is COASTING" lines | 44, 54, 55 | 2 |
| Recovery climbs | 2, 0, 1 | 0 |

**What the ring fit itself did in that flight**, from the node's own log: of the frames it was sent, 1391 were taken below 4 m between the descent and the release and looked at, and 1100 of them (79%) gave rings; 3 frames had no pose of their moment; the rest were above 4 m. (A further 168 frames below 4 m were looked at during the landing at home, where there is no target.) The estimator's track against the truth: p95 0.08 m at 15 m, 0.06 m at 6 m, 0.04 m at 2.4 m (limits 0.30, 0.15, 0.08), with a confirmed track in all 514 samples at 2.4 m.

An earlier flight of the same scenario, before the review's changes (the cut-box rule still applied to the ring fit; unlevelled fits still published), gave 0.199 m, 50 s and no lost track.

In a 7 m/s wind the vehicle holds a tilt of about 8°, which at 2.2 m moves the hard-mounted camera's view by 0.3 m; the picture is 2.0 m tall there, so the 1.5 m target's red runs into the picture's edge about half the time, and the estimator does not take a cut box as a centre. The ring fit's centre does not come from a box, and the estimator does not put it through the cut-box rule.

## What to keep in mind

- **The scenario is in no gate, and nothing in it fails if the ring fit is silent**: the same hold has finished OK without it. What shows the ring fit working is the node's counts and the absence of lost-track lines, read by a person, not a pass line.
- One flight with the ring fit on the committed code, one seed. The three "without" flights had three different controller settings (Kp 0.6, Kp 1.0, Kp 1.0 with the lead); the losses of the track were the same in all three, the hold error is not a like-for-like comparison to the centimetre.
- The rendered targets and the sim's are clean red on white. A printed target under the sun, with shadows and a dull red, is the bench check (Needs a person, CEN-13).
- The node was not timed in the flight. The fit takes 16 ms on a 640 x 360 frame on this laptop; the aircraft's frames are four times the pixels.
- The ring fit's height gate is the pose's height above home, not above the ground.
