## What to read with it

- **The flags.** The gate is flown with PLT-13's flags on. The waypoint flights' 3 s hold is a setting the 2026 mission has too; what the flag adds in these flights is the timeout, which did not fire. The 2026 mission was not flown beside them here.
- **The time trial is in AUTO.** Every time trial uploaded its mission and flew it in AUTO, passing each waypoint within 2.0 m. That needs MAVROS's `waypoint` plugin, which these wall-time sim flights have and the aircraft's MAVROS config does not: on the aircraft the flag would fly in GUIDED until the lead allows the plugin (PLT-13, "Needs a person"). The 2026 GUIDED time trial, flown once on this circuit, took 71.34 s and passed every waypoint within 0.83 m (`docs/results/2026-11-23-gps-missions.md`).
- **Wind.** Moderate wind cost the time trial 0.5 to 3.3 s and moved the waypoint flights' closest approach from 0.02 m to 0.20 to 0.31 m.
- The calm flights of one scenario are near copies of each other (62.95 to 62.96 s): the seed changes the wind's gusts, and in calm air little else.
