- **The coordinate error** was 3.6 to 7.2 m. One flight (seed 4, 7.2 m) had to search; the others saw the bucket from the pickup's coordinates at 15 m.
- **"Every failure ends in a climb and RTL."** No flight failed, so the rule was met by ten flights that ended landed beside the bucket. What a failure does is shown elsewhere: the mission goes up and 2 m aside and then fails, and recovery's abort mode is LAND, not RTL (REC-7: an RTL refused for want of a position is asked for again for ever). The gate's wording says RTL; the lead decides whether it stands.
- **"Ended safely"** is the judge's new detail: down and disarmed, the last 5 s at no more than 1.5 m/s.
