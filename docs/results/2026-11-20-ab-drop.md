- With the flags on the drop takes longer: 12 s in calm air, and 16 to 58 s in wind, where the descent waits whenever the vehicle is more than 0.20 m off the track.
- No release or flight below 1.83 m on either side; the lowest gated release was at 2.41 m.
- **The one FAIL is a check, not the drop.** In wind, seed 1, the projection check (FND-05's line: 0.30 m at the 95th percentile at 15 m) read 0.304 m over 187 detections; the drop itself released on its gates 0.090 m from the target. The harness first counted that flight as a failure of the flags-on side and its verdict was FAIL ("wind: 1 failure with the flags on, 0 with them off"). That count was wrong in kind: the projection, estimator and height checks run only beside flags-on flights, so a flags-off flight can never be marked that way. The harness now counts a flight as failed when it releases nothing or does not end as a flight should, and lists a missed check apart, as above. The check's miss stands as recorded, and is the same line that the wave's Decisions already report as close to its limit in wind. **Which reading of "failures" stands is the lead's to confirm** (PROGRESS.md, "Needs a person"): under the first, this A/B is a FAIL; under the second, a PASS. A gate (`sim/gate.py`) would count this flight as failed.
