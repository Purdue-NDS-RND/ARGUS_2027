# Wind in the sim (SIM-03)

Measured on 18 Nov 2026 (UTC) on a club laptop, on `platform/build-2027` at `9fc2bae` plus the SIM-03 change. Sim image `argus-sim:humble`.

**Result: PASS.** Hovering in a steady 5 m/s wind, the vehicle's tilt (Gazebo's true attitude) was 4.10 degrees against atan(F / mg) = 4.10 degrees, well inside the 20% line. The 2026 delivery, flown once in the moderate level (4 m/s with 1 m/s gusts), landed 0.04 m from the target.

## The pass flight

```
$ sim/run_scenarios.sh hover/wind_5 --seeds 1 --variants off
| hover/wind_5 | 1 | OK | 1.00 | held within 0.18 m for 74 s | not run |  |  |
| Wind | mean 5.0 m/s, 5.0 to 5.0 m/s, from 270 degrees, over 1784 updates |
| Hover tilt in the wind | PASS: 4.10 degrees against atan(F / mg) = 4.10 degrees (force 1.23 N, mass 1.755 kg, limit 20%) |
```

`sim/hover.py` takes off to 10 m, holds for 40 s in GUIDED and returns; `sim/wind.py` applies F = ½ ρ CdA |v_rel| v_rel (CdA 0.08 m²) to the vehicle 20 times a second of sim time. From the run's `wind.json` and `wind.jsonl`:

| --- | --- |
| Updates while hovering (above 2 m, slower than 0.5 m/s) | 1446 |
| Mean drag force on the vehicle | 1.232 N (½ × 1.225 × 0.08 × 5² = 1.225 N for a vehicle that is still) |
| Mean tilt; atan(F / mg) with m = 1.755 kg | 4.101°; 4.096° (0.1% apart; the line is 20%) |
| Tilt during the hold: lowest; median; highest | 3.06°; 4.08°; 4.90° |

| Time between updates (median) | 0.051 s of sim time |

| Real-time factor while armed | 1.00 |

The wind is from the west, so the force is toward the east; the autopilot leans into it and holds its place.

## A mission in the moderate level

```
$ sim/run_scenarios.sh delivery/wind_moderate --seeds 1 --variants off
| delivery/wind_moderate | 1 | OK | 16.20 | 0.04 m from the target | not run |  |  |
| Wind | mean 3.8 m/s, 0.7 to 6.4 m/s, from 225 degrees, over 3125 updates |
| Hover tilt in the wind | PASS: 2.60 degrees against atan(F / mg) = 2.44 degrees (force 0.73 N, mass 1.755 kg, limit 20%) |
```

The 2026 delivery, every flag off, in 4 m/s from the south-west with 1 m/s gusts: the wind ranged from 0.7 to 6.4 m/s over the flight and the touchdown was 0.04 m from the target, as in calm air (0.02 to 0.08 m at gate G0). The tilt row is from the stretches where the mission hovered; with gusts the tilt runs 6% above the tilt of the mean force, as it should when force goes with the square of the speed and the vehicle is also correcting its position.
