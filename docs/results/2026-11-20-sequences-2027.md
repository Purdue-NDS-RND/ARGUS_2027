# The 2027 sequences, flown in the sim with their live tuning and the 2027 flight flags on

Measured on 20 Nov 2026 (UTC) on a club laptop, on `platform/build-2027` at `390f027` plus the CEN-10 change. Sim image `argus-sim:humble`. One flight per scenario, calm air, flags on, sim time.

**Result.** The sim copies of `package_drop_2027.yaml` and `package_delivery_2027.yaml`, which carry the live files' heights, rates and timings, flew their missions with eight of the ten flags each file declares on (all but `time_health_guard` and `enforce_safety_limits`): the drop released on its gates at 2.54 m, 0.026 m from the target; the delivery set down 0.029 m from the target and released on contact.

## Commands

```
sim/run_scenarios.sh drop/live_tuning_2027 --seeds 1 --variants on
sim/run_scenarios.sh delivery/live_tuning_2027 --seeds 1 --variants on
```

```
| drop/live_tuning_2027 | 1 | not run |  |  | OK | 17.47 | 0.03 m from the target |
| delivery/live_tuning_2027 | 1 | not run |  |  | OK | 16.21 | 0.03 m from the target |
```

Flags on in both: `use_frame_time_pose`, `use_target_estimator`, `use_height_estimator`, `centre_on_estimator`, `centring_retune`, `use_ring_fit`, `smart_reacquire`, and `drop_release_gates` or `delivery_soft_set_down`; node flags `target_cv.stamp_from_image`, `target_cv.detection_contract`, `ring_fit.ring_fit`. Not on: `time_health_guard` and `enforce_safety_limits`, which no scenario turns on. No sim flight has `time_health_guard` on yet: `sim/scenarios/delivery/time_base.yaml` records why (a frame is 250 to 280 ms old against the guard's 250 ms, so with it on the 2026 delivery never got down). `enforce_safety_limits` is a check at start-up, which `test_safety_limits.py` runs over every shipped sequence. So `time_guard_hold_timeout_s` in the 2027 files was not exercised here. Both are fake drops (a sim copy never commands a release), scored at the vehicle's place at the release. Variant off of these scenarios is every flag false, which is the 2026 live file's flight (`drop/live_tuning`, `delivery/live_tuning`, flown for CEN-11); it was not flown again.

| Delivery: handoff to the last descent | 6 m, within 0.2 m | 8 m, within 3 m |
| Delivery: descent rate | 0.35 m/s | 0.4 m/s |
| Delivery: lock time | 1.0 s | 0.2 s |
