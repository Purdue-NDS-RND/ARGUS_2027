# The replay harness on recorded sim flights (SIM-09)

Measured on 18 Nov 2026 (UTC) on a club laptop, on `platform/build-2027` at `89f87b5` plus the SIM-09 change. Sim image `argus-sim:humble`.

**Result.** A sim flight was recorded with `--record`, read back without ROS, and replayed through the estimator in plain Python: the replayed bullseye track is the one the flight's own estimator published (0.001 m apart at the median, 0.066 m at most, over 672 ticks), and running the replay twice gives the same bytes. The same bag played through `ros2 bag play` by `play.launch.py` gives one `/clock`, the original stamps, and the flight's outputs under `/recorded`. The launch first checks that no MAVROS is live in the ROS domain, and does not start if one is or if it cannot tell. Every bag here is sqlite3: the sim image has no MCAP plugin, so MCAP was written and read only by the replay's own code (see "Not shown").

The launch file itself, in a container of the sim image with a small publisher standing in for the camera driver and MAVROS:

```
$ ros2 launch sim/replay/record.launch.py out:=/tmp/launchbag storage:=sqlite3 tap_images:=/camera/image
{'/argus/camera/down/camera_info': 40, '/mavros/local_position/pose': 40, '/replay/frame_seen/camera/image': 40}
```

Both processes started (the recorder and the taps), and the bag holds every one of the 40 messages sent on each topic: camera info under the 2027 driver's name, poses, and one header per frame.

Both bags were recorded while the list still held `/mavros/rc/out` (840 messages in the baseline's, where the sim's default MAVROS loads every plugin; none in the other, where the 2027 plugin list does not load `rc_io`). The topic has since been taken off the list: see Decisions.

## The layouts against ROS

The replay decodes messages with its own table of layouts. In a container of the sim image:

```
$ python3 sim/replay/check_defs.py
33 of 33 layouts match ROS; 0 differ; 0 not installed
```

Each type's fields are the installed message's, ROS's bytes decode to the values that went in, and the bytes written here are read back by ROS unchanged.

## `core`: the estimator on the recorded flight

```
$ python3 sim/replay/core.py <run folder>/bag --truth-file <run folder>/truth.json --out replay_metrics.json
1719 frames with detections, 1613 ticks; states of the bullseye track: {'COASTING': 86, 'CONFIRMED': 677, 'LOST': 400, 'TENTATIVE': 11, 'none': 439}
error of the CONFIRMED bullseye track: median 0.010 m, p95 0.044 m, max 0.167 m over 677 ticks
NIS: mean 0.09, 0.0% above 9.21 over 1311 measurements
time to lock 0.34 s; LOST for 40.00 s; without a lock between the first and the last 17.90 s
against the tracks the flight itself published: median 0.001 m, max 0.066 m apart over 672 ticks; CONFIRMED only in the replay 5, only in the flight 3
capture to heard: median 42 ms, p95 100 ms, max 447 ms over 1719 frames
```

| | Replayed from the bag | The flight itself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ticks with a CONFIRMED bullseye track | 677 | 675 published |
| Distance between the two tracks where both were CONFIRMED | median 0.001 m, 95th percentile 0.006 m, maximum 0.066 m over 672 ticks | |
| Error against the truth | median 0.010 m, 95th percentile 0.044 m over the whole flight | `estimator_check`: 95th percentile 0.093 m at 15 m, 0.019 m at 6 m, 0.009 m at 2.4 m |
| Measurements accepted; rejected; boxes cut by the image's edge | 1311; 0; 333 | |

- **Deterministic.** A second run wrote a byte-identical `replay_metrics.json` (`sha256` `151ead4be398fd29…` both times).
- The eight ticks where only one of the two had a CONFIRMED track are the edges of coasting: the replay steps the feed at the recorder's arrival times and reads the tracks on its own 100 ms ticks, both a few milliseconds from the executor's. The largest distance (0.066 m) is in the first tick after the lock, before the filter has settled.
- **The mean NIS is 0.09**, where a filter whose noise model matched its measurements would give about 2. In the sim the estimator is told far more noise than there is (a degree of attitude error against a simulated attitude that is nearly exact), so it trusts each frame less than it could. Nothing is changed for it: the noise numbers are the spec's and are for the aircraft. On a field bag this number says whether they fit.
- "Without a lock" is mostly the 5 s on the ground and the climb away, when the bullseye fills the frame. LOST for 40 s is the return and landing, after the mission has switched the detector off.

## Not shown

- **MCAP on a real bag.** The image has rosbag2 with sqlite3 only. `record.launch.py` and the sim's recorder ask for MCAP with the `fastwrite` preset (plain records, no zstd) when the plugin is there; the replay's MCAP reader has read only what its own writer wrote. The person step is under "Needs a person".
- **The `ros` mode with nodes under test.** It was played and listened to; no detector or executor was run against it. There are no frames in the bag for a detector, and the estimator's input (the contract detections) is one of the flight's outputs, which the launch moves aside by default (`keep_outputs:=true` leaves them).
- **The aircraft.** `record.launch.py` has not run on the Jetson. In the sim's flights the recording goes through `--record`, which builds the same `ros2 bag record` command and starts it as a runner side tool; the launch file was run on its own, as above. What a frame tap costs on the Jetson is not known; the launch file taps nothing unless told to.
- **Frames on disk.** Nothing writes the 4K JPEG frames and index during a recording yet.
- **The error at the release.** No recorded flight has it: nothing in the bag marks the release.
- **Flow and marker metrics.** The flow code and the marker tracker do not exist yet.
