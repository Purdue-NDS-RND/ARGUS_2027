# Centring on the estimator: holds at 6 m and 2 m, and a delivery, in the sim

Measured on 20 Nov 2026 (UTC) on a club laptop, on `platform/build-2027` at `df82141` plus the CEN-01 change. Sim image `argus-sim:humble`. One flight of each scenario and variant, seed 1, calm, fake release. "On" is `use_frame_time_pose`, `use_target_estimator` and `centre_on_estimator`, on sim time; "off" is the 2026 centring on the raw detection, on wall time, with the same sim demo tuning (gain 0.25, tolerance 0.22 m).

**Result.** Held over a 1.5 m bullseye for 20 s by `TRACK_AND_DESCEND`'s centring alone, the vehicle's true distance from the target's centre at the 95th percentile was 0.02 m with centring on the estimator, at 6 m and at 2 m, against 0.07 m with the 2026 centring, which stops correcting inside its 0.15 m deadband. Gate G1 allows 0.20 m in moderate wind; these flights are calm. With a release point 0.30 m ahead of the vehicle and 0.10 m to its left, that point was held 0.02 m from the centre while the vehicle sat 0.32 m away, as it should. The delivery with centring on touched down 0.01 m from the centre.

The judge places that point from Gazebo's true position and true heading, which the trace now records, not from anything the mission computed. A wrong sign, or a yaw taken in the wrong convention, would have put the judged point 0.3 to 0.6 m off. One heading was flown; other headings are covered by unit tests.

## What happened on the way

- **First attempt: neither hold got past acquisition, flags on or off.** The scenarios ask the drop mission for 20 s centred, and the mission asks the same 20 s of the acquisition at 15 m. With the 1.5 m, eight-ring bullseye a ring is 2.4 pixels wide at 15 m; the 2026 detector's blur turns red and white to pink and it reports "no red pixels after thresholding" for seconds at a time. The holds now use four rings. The delivery scenarios, which need the target at 15 m for one second, were not affected.
- **A 6 m hold with flags on was first failed by the estimator's check, not by the hold** (0.021 m at p95): on its way home the vehicle landed through 2 to 2.8 m, which the check read as 19 samples at 2.4 m with no confirmed track. The check now counts samples only within 5 m of the target and does not judge a band the flight was never in. The flight in the table is the one flown after that.
- **Descents pause on COASTING.** "not descending: the bullseye track is COASTING, not CONFIRMED" was logged once in the first delivery flight and once in the first 2 m hold's descent, and not in the 6 m holds, nor in any of the four flights on the committed code. The line is written at most every 2 s; each time the descent resumed by itself. Recorded under Decisions, as the lead asked; the rule is unchanged.
- **The first 2 m holds were at 1.97 m and 1.89 m of true height**, 0.14 m and 0.06 m above the 1.83 m floor: the 2026 mission stops its descent on the flight controller's height, which reads 0.19 m high in this sim, and the vehicle coasts a little past it. The scenario's drop height is now 2.2 m with its 0.3 m tolerance, so nothing under 1.9 m is commanded, and the hold was at 2.16 m. No run went under the floor (`flew_low` false in all).
- **After the first review the centring changed** (a track that may not be descended on is never "centred"; one approach keeps to one track), and the four flag-on scenarios were flown again. The tables are those flights; the earlier ones gave 0.017 m and 0.016 m for the holds and 0.01 m for the delivery.

## What to keep in mind

- One flight each, one seed, calm air, the sim demo's gain. Wind and the retuned controller are CEN-02; gate G1 is judged on those flights, not these.
- The "off" flights are on wall time and the "on" flights on sim time, as every flags-off and flags-on pair in this sim.
- A four-ring 1.5 m target is not the competition's. It is what lets one target be seen steadily at 15 m and still fit inside the image at 2 m in this sim; below about 4 m the real answer is the ring fit (CEN-13).
