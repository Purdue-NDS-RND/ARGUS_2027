## Why the gate stays FAIL

G2's wind line (median 0.30 m, with a real package) cannot be met by anything built so far. Aiming the vehicle at the target leaves the package's drift in the fall, which is CEN-08's job (the release offset model, P2, not built); and a package heavy enough to fall straighter is one the sim's vehicle cannot carry in that wind. The gate was not flown a second time: neither is something a re-run changes. The scenario keeps the 0.2 kg package, with which it can at least be flown.

What the gate did establish for CEN-04: a real package released on the gates in calm air lands 0.02 to 0.04 m from the target, and in strong wind the vehicle is 0.08 m from the target at the release, with no release or descent below 1.83 m when the vehicle can carry what it has.
