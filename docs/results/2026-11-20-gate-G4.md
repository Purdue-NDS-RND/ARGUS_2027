## Why it is a FAIL, and what it is not

- In every flight the list has eight rows, digits 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7 and 8, every one with the right ID and
  within 0.08 m of its marker. The two markers not listed are the 6 and the 9, in all three flights: 24 of 30.
- The sim's marker model (`build_marker_sdf`) has nothing that says which way up it is, and its 6 is its 9
  turned half round: the same picture. The reader answers neither from a crop, the vote is told "6 or 9" for
  both tracks, and with two such tracks the assignment has nothing to choose by, so both stay unread
  (LOC-6, LOC-8). Whether the rules' marker has a mark for it is organiser question 8.
- It is not a fault in finding or placing the markers: all ten are tracked and counted in every flight
  ("8 markers, 2 more unread" is what the ground station is told), and the position, height and
  list-before-landing rules are met with room to spare.
- Under the sim judge's scoring a wrong ID costs half a point and a right one earns a point, so listing the
  two at a coin's toss would be worth a quarter of a point a marker on average, and the gate would still not
  be met by it. The reader does not guess; that is the lead's to change.

## The first run, and what it found

The gate was flown twice. The first run listed 10 of 30 (3, 4 and 4 rows), with one wrong ID:

| Seed | Listed | Correct | Wrong ID | Rows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3 | 3 | 0 | 0, 2, 4 |
| 2 | 4 | 4 | 0 | 0, 2, 4, 6 |
| 3 | 4 | 3 | 1 | 0, 2, 4, 9 (the 6) |

Two faults, both fixed before the run above (`7601ee7`, `da98c47`):

- The oracle boxed every marker at its side's width. A marker lying at an angle in the picture is wider, so its
  crop cut the plate's corners off and the reader found no plate: only the markers lying square to the camera
  (headings 0, 90 and 180 degrees) were read. The oracle now boxes a turned marker whole, as a detector would.
- A reading that was not sure kept the probabilities of whichever way up scored better, which for a 6 or a 9
  is a leaning that means nothing; over a track's sightings the leanings added up to a 6 (right by luck) and a
  9 (wrong). Every reading of a marker with no cue now gives 6 and 9 the same chance.

Single crops: in the run above the reader answered 508 crops and gave no answer on 413; no frame's crops were
skipped for time, and the pipeline held the camera's 10 frames a second in all three flights.
